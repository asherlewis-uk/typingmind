1. Brand Identity

Tagline: "Connect any model. Orchestrate everything."

The nexumChat identity centers on a connected-node "N" mark representing neural networks, orchestration, and the hub-and-spoke architecture of the platform. The gradient flows blue (models) through purple (agents) to coral (workflows).

5. Deployment Checklist

  1. Config text updates: manifest.json, browserconfig.xml, index.html, capacitor.config.json, build.gradle, Info.plist, project.pbxproj, package.json
  2. Copy core assets: favicons, PWA icons, marketing images to src/
  3. iOS: Copy AppIcon-512@2x.png to xcassets, copy splash screens
  4. Android: Copy mipmap-* and drawable-* resources
  5. Regenerate: Run npx cap sync to propagate splash screens
  6. White-label: Copy to src/assets/white-label/
  7. Verify: Check all manifest paths resolve, no stale brand text remains
